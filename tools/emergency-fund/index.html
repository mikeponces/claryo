How it works

Months covered is the cash you keep for emergencies divided by your average monthly expenses. Count only money set aside for emergencies. Cash already meant for bills you know are coming, or for a purchase you have decided on, is spent already.

  • 3 months is the minimum. From here the fund is on track.
  • 6 months is the target.
  • 9 and 12 months are buffers past the target.

Below 3 months the fund needs attention. The status compares the money itself, never the rounded months.

How to use your results

Below 3 months, the fund comes first. Set part of each paycheck aside for it until it reaches the minimum, then keep going to 6.

From 3 to 6 months the fund is on track. Months past 6 are a buffer.

Work it out again when your expenses change. A higher rent or a new loan payment raises the target with them.
