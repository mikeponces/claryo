How it works

Your monthly contribution is your monthly income times your investment rate. Each month, what you hold grows by the annual return divided by 12, and that month's contribution is added at the end of the month.

You contribute for the years you choose. After that no new money goes in, and the balance keeps growing at the same return.

Projected wealth is three parts: what you hold today, everything you put in, and the returns on both. The multiple compares that total with what you put in, the starting balance included.

In today's money divides the total by one plus inflation, once for every year. The monthly income is that figure times your withdrawal rate, divided by 12, so it is income in what money buys now, not in future prices.

How to use your results

The projection assumes the same return and the same inflation every year. Real markets move up and down, so read the total as an estimate of where this path leads, not a promise.

Your investment rate and the years you keep investing are the two parts you set. Try a higher rate or a longer horizon to see how far each one moves the total.

Compare the monthly income, in today's money, with what your household spends now. Move the year to follow the path: the gap between the total and the dashed line is what inflation takes from it.
