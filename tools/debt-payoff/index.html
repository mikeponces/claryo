How it works

Each debt keeps its own monthly payment. Every month, interest is added at the debt's yearly rate divided by 12, and then each payment is taken off.

  • When any debt has a rate, the order is highest rate first. With no rates at all, it is smallest balance first.
  • When a debt is paid off, its payment moves to the first card in that order that still has a balance. A loan keeps its fixed payment and never takes extra.
  • A debt with no monthly payment is left out and named. If a payment cannot clear its debt within 50 years, that debt is never paid off at that payment.

Each debt also falls into one of four kinds, by rate and size. A rate of 20% or more is costly, and a balance of ₱31,623 or more is big. Those lines are the same in every currency.

    How to use your results

    Pay every debt its monthly payment. When one is paid off, move its payment to the next card in the order instead of spending it. That is what clears the later debts sooner.

    A debt that is never paid off needs a larger payment. At its current payment the interest keeps pace with it, or it would take more than 50 years.

    Each debt's kind, from 1 · Small & costly to 4 · Big & cheap, shows where it sits by rate and size. The guide explains why the kinds are paid in that order.
