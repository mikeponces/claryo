How it works

Your investment rate is what you invest each month divided by your monthly income. Count everything that goes into investments: retirement accounts, funds and stocks, and stores of wealth such as gold.

  • At or above your target, the rate is on track.
  • At half the target or more, it needs attention.
  • Below half the target, it is at risk.

Investing nothing is a rate of 0%, and it is at risk.

How to use your results

Below half your target the rate is at risk. Investing a larger share of each paycheck moves it fastest.

Pick the target for where you are. 10% builds the habit, 15% is the standard floor for long-term retirement security, and 25% or more shortens the timeline to financial independence.

Work it out again when your income or your investing changes.
