The December beta

Plan your real December paydays in Claryo before it launches, and tell us where you got stuck.

Who it’s for

Households that run on a payday, in the Philippines or abroad, and hold at least two of these: a savings account, MP2 or investments, insurance, and a loan or credit card. With two or more, the Cash Flow, Protection and Growth tabs have something of yours to show.

You can test on an iPhone, through TestFlight, or on an Android phone, through Google Play’s internal test.

What you’ll do

Four missions, one for each stretch of the holidays. These are planned dates, from 1 December to 5 January.

  1. 1–7 Dec Install Claryo, set it up, and plan your 15 December payday.
  2. 8–15 Dec Carry out your 15 December plan: mark each line done as the money moves.
  3. 16–24 Dec Plan your 13th month pay.
  4. 25 Dec–5 Jan Plan your 30 December payday, then copy December’s plan into January.

What we ask

Post what you find on the feedback board, in Settings › Support › Feedback board. On iPhone, TestFlight’s screenshot feedback reaches us too.

Answer two short surveys, on day 7 and on 5 January. Each one has four questions.

We’ll ask five testers for a 20-minute call in the first week. On the call you turn on Hide Amounts and share your screen while you plan a payday, so the founder sees where you hesitate and never sees a figure.

There is no group chat to keep up with over the holidays.

What it costs

The beta is free, on TestFlight or Google Play’s internal test.

Claryo is a paid download when it launches. Testers pay the same price as everyone else, and there are no promo codes.

How to apply

Email us with three things: your phone (iPhone or Android), how you’re paid (monthly, twice a month or irregularly), and which of savings, MP2 or investments, insurance, and a loan or card you hold. The beta takes 15 to 20 testers.
