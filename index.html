Questions

How is Claryo different from a budgeting or expense-tracking app?

Most apps record each purchase after it happens. In Claryo, you plan each paycheck before it arrives and track your balances once a month, which keeps the bigger picture in view. You direct your money instead of letting it direct you.

Claryo also goes past the budget. It covers three pillars, built in order: Cash Flow (your cash reserves and debt), Protection (life, health and medical insurance) and Growth (your investments and the income they pay). Together they show whether all your household's financial bases are covered.

If Claryo is for breadwinners, can I use it if I'm single or have no dependents?

Yes. To us, a breadwinner is anyone who takes responsibility for their household's financial future, and a household can be just you. If you put in the effort, think long-term and want to keep improving, Claryo is for you.

Can I use Claryo if my income is irregular?

Yes. We recommend a fixed payday schedule even when your income is irregular, because the same paycheck on the same days makes your plan consistent and predictable. An employer sets an employee's salary. If you're self-employed, you set your own.

Estimate what you'll earn this year and pay yourself the same amount each payday, sized to that estimate. When a month falls short, withdraw the difference from your cash reserves. When a large payment comes in, replenish your reserves again.

Do I need to know a lot about finance to use it?

No. Claryo tracks the few numbers that matter most to your household, in order: plan your paycheck first, then build Cash Flow, Protection and Growth one at a time, moving on as your understanding grows. Each tab also has a short tutorial. You don't need to know a lot today, but you will learn as you go.

Is my financial data safe?

Claryo never asks for bank logins, account or card numbers, or government IDs. There are no ads and no tracking, and your data is never sold. What you enter is stored on Supabase servers, encrypted in transit and at rest, and each account can read only its own data.

How do I get help, report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email us at claryoapp@gmail.com, or post on the feedback board in Settings › Support, where you can also vote on other people's posts. Other users can see board posts, but not who wrote them.

How much does Claryo cost?

You pay once when you download it, and every feature is open from the first launch. There's no subscription, no in-app purchases, and no free version or trial. Updates are included. The App Store and Google Play show the price for your country.
