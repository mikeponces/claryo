Claryo: Paycheck System

Plan your paycheck. Secure your financial future — in the right order.

Most breadwinners don't have an income problem. They have a conversion problem: income arrives, expenses absorb it, and nothing builds. Claryo is the system that closes that gap, paycheck by paycheck.

Built for breadwinners

You carry financial responsibility for a household, on one income or two. Claryo is for people who are ready to do the work.

No streaks. No badges. No cheerleading.

Budget

Plan every paycheck

Save, Repay, Spend, Earmark, Invest. You plan every paycheck before it arrives.

Cash Flow

Cash has four jobs

Your emergency fund, recurring expenses, one-off goals, and cash with no job yet. Only one of them is savings.

Protection

When ten times income runs out

Ten times income, ₱12.0M, covers a ₱900K-a-year household for 16½ of 20 years. The red marks the years it doesn't reach.

Growth

Your portfolio, core and satellites

The investments you track, drawn as a core with satellites around it.

Your investment rate is the share of income that becomes assets.

10%The floor
15%Covers retirement if you start early
25%Opens options before 65
40–50%Financial independence on an unconventional timeline

Start where you need to

Claryo is comprehensive by design. Cash Flow, Protection and Growth can each be switched off, and your paycheck stays on. Pick your starting point on first run, build the habit there, then add the next tab in Settings whenever you're ready. Nothing is locked.
