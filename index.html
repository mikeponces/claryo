Claryo: Paycheck System

Plan your paycheck. Secure your financial future — in the right order.

Most breadwinners don't have an income problem. They have a conversion problem: income arrives, expenses absorb it, and nothing builds. Claryo is the system that closes that gap, paycheck by paycheck.

The right order

Financial security has three layers, and the order matters. Each one rests on the one below it, and all three rest on the paycheck.

Layer 3Growth

Invest aggressively once the foundation holds. Your investment rate on target, your portfolio income building.

Layer 2Protection

Life and health cover that meets its benchmark, so the household is covered if something happens to you.

Layer 1Cash Flow

Debt moving down, expenses stable, cash reserves funded.

The baseYour paycheck

Planned before it arrives. Savings and investments are allocated first; expenses follow. Unallocated income is an incomplete plan, not a safety buffer.

Most apps skip the foundation and go straight to investment tracking. Claryo follows the order: you can't responsibly chase your investment rate on empty reserves.

Your investment rate

What percentage of your income becomes assets that generate future cash flow? That number is the leading indicator of financial independence. Claryo tracks it against a target you set, every paycheck.

10%The floor
15%Covers retirement if you start early
25%Opens options before 65
40–50%Financial independence on an unconventional timeline

You set the target

The benchmarks come from established personal finance frameworks. Claryo tracks whether your paychecks are getting there.

Portfolio income

Your investment rate is the leading indicator. Portfolio income is the lagging one: what the rate becomes, compounded. Claryo shows your FI ratio, portfolio income as a share of monthly expenses. When it reaches 100%, work is optional.

Built for breadwinners

Breadwinners carry financial responsibility for a household. They do this work because people depend on them getting it right. Claryo was built for them: the person managing one income or two, solo or with a partner, who needs to know the household is covered.

For people who are ready

Claryo requires engagement. The paycheck planning is real work, and the signals demand honest input. There is no automated version of this.

A different kind of tool

If you've tried other budget planners and found them shallow, gamified or patronizing, this is not that. A clear system and the data to make good decisions.

No streaks. No badges. No cheerleading.

One price. Every feature.

Claryo is a paid app. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no paywall. You buy it once and everything's included. The price is a filter: it selects for people ready to do the work.
