Support

Contact us

Email us. It reaches the founder, and replies come from the same address.

hello@claryoapp.com

Delete your account

In the app, go to Settings › Delete Account. It deletes your login and every row of your data at once.

Without the app, email hello@claryoapp.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done. If you use Hide My Email, write from any address and include your relay address.

Feedback board

Post problems and ideas on the feedback board in Settings › Support › Feedback board, and vote on other people’s posts. Other users can see board posts, but not who wrote them.
